Gift Vouchers — quieter treatments

Six ways to mention gift vouchers on the Restaurant & Takeaway pages without giving them the same weight as the info cards. Option 0 shows the current treatment for comparison.

Option 0 — current

Full card, same weight as the others

What's on the pages now. Fourth card in the row, styled identically to Opening hours / Facilities.

Opening hours

Open every day, except Christmas day, Boxing day and New Year's day

Monday – Sunday 12pm–9pmLast orders at 8pm.

Facilities

High chairs & baby-changing facilities available.

Fully air-conditioned. Free Wi-Fi. Free car parking.

Gift Vouchers

Available in £5 denominations and redeemable in both the restaurant & takeaway against food & drink.

Ideal as a birthday present or leaving gift — please just ask for them in the restaurant or the takeaway.
