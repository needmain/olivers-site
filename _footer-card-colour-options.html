Footer address cards — colour options

Five card backgrounds, shown on the navy footer. Each has a gold top border and a gold dot above the location name, as chosen. Text contrast passes AA comfortably in all five (14.8:1 down to 11.5:1), so legibility isn't the deciding factor — this is purely about which reads best to the eye.
